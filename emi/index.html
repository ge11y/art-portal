Story

Two hundred and twenty-two drawings.
One hand.

“Every piece of art is a 1/1 hand drawn by me.” — OgBe, announcing the drop

OgBe Meji is a multidisciplinary artist and musician working out of San Juan, Puerto Rico. After two decades as a professional musician and a run of hand-drawn collections inscribed on Bitcoin, EMI brings his line to Robinhood: 222 individual drawings, no two alike, every one made by hand.

The collection minted on September 3, 2026 and sold out the same afternoon. All 222 pieces are revealed, and you can put any of them on either ground in the studio above.
